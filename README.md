# us_jobs

**Goal:** Find and apply for jobs at these 20 companies to secure a US **H-1B visa**.

Most H-1B applicants get sponsored through one of these employers. This list combines
US tech giants, consulting/finance firms, and Indian IT services companies — the top
sponsors of H-1B petitions over recent years (FY2021–FY2025), based on USCIS data
analyzed by NFAP and reported in Forbes, H1B Grader, CareerNomics, ZeroPerks, and
Indian Express.

## How to use this repo

1. Pick 2–3 companies from the list that match your skills (software, data, finance, consulting).
2. Search each company's careers page for roles you qualify for (H-1B-friendly).
3. Track your applications and interviews in `APPLICATIONS.md`.
4. Update this list as new data comes out (H-1B rules change frequently).

## Important notes on H-1B data

- **Indian IT firms' share has dropped sharply.** In FY2025, only 3 Indian companies
  made the top 25 — a ~70% fall from FY2015 and 37% fewer than FY2024 (NFAP/USCIS).
- **New vs. continuing petitions differ.** Amazon leads *new* approvals; TCS/Amazon
  lead *continuing* (extensions). Rankings shift depending on the metric.
- **H-1B is employer-sponsored** — you cannot self-petition, and it's subject to an
  annual cap/lottery (85,000/year).

## Related visa types for Indians working in the US

| Visa | Best for |
|------|----------|
| **H-1B** | Specialty occupations (your main path) — employer-sponsored |
| **L-1** | Intracompany transfer (already at a firm with US + India offices) |
| **O-1** | Extraordinary ability (science, business, arts) |
| **F-1 / OPT** | Students already studying in the US (short-term work authorization) |
